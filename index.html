№ 002 FLAGSHIP PROJECT

Project RAPTORM86

A six-degree-of-freedom fixed-wing flight dynamics engine written in Rust and exposed to Godot 4 through a native GDExtension. Quaternion rigid-body dynamics, RK4 integration, the 1976 US Standard Atmosphere, nonlinear post-stall aerodynamics, wind shear and Dryden turbulence.

The wing section below is the same physics the engine uses: drag the plate to set the angle of attack and the lift coefficient, drag coefficient and flow state follow the real aerodynamic model, not a canned animation.

№ 003 PEER-REVIEWED RESEARCH

Dueling Double DQN, in Rust

Deep reinforcement learning for autonomous fixed-wing UAV control, implemented without an external ML library and integrated with Godot 4 through GDExtension.

PUBLISHED · JAMCS

A Rust-Implemented Dueling Double DQN for Fixed-Wing Autonomous Flight

Saugat Chaudhary Tharu et al. · JAMCS 41(8), 163–178, 2026 · DOI: 10.9734/jamcs/2026/v41i82191

A Dueling + Double DQN combines prioritised experience replay and three-step returns in a 512→256 hidden configuration — 142,088 parameters for the 16-dimensional input case and seven discrete actions.

№ 005 ABOUT

Learning by doing

Passionate AI/ML developer with a focus on computer vision, intelligent systems and real-world problem solving. I believe in learning by doing and embrace the journey of continuous improvement.

I work with cutting-edge technologies including Rust-based ML frameworks like Burn, traditional Python ML stacks, and full-stack development tools. My goal is to build intelligent systems that make a real impact.

My published research — a Rust-implemented Dueling Double DQN for autonomous fixed-wing flight in JAMCS (2026) — is exactly the kind of problem I love: physics, reinforcement learning, and a game engine (Godot 4 through GDExtension) all wired together and documented end to end.

Outside the terminal I'm into flight sims, tinkering with autonomous drones, and pushing browser visualisation far past "good enough". I break things on purpose to learn faster, and I always leave a trail of notes behind.
