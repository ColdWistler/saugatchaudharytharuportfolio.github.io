RESEARCH

$ // peer-reviewed paper — Journal of Advances in Mathematics and Computer Science
PUBLISHED · JAMCS

A Rust-Implemented Dueling Double DQN for Fixed-Wing Autonomous Flight

Saugat Chaudhary Tharu et al. · JAMCS 41(8), 163–178, 2026 · DOI: 10.9734/jamcs/2026/v41i82191

Deep reinforcement learning for autonomous fixed-wing UAV control, implemented entirely in Rust without an external ML library and integrated with Godot 4 through GDExtension. A Dueling + Double DQN combines prioritized experience replay and three-step returns in a 512→256 hidden configuration — 142,088 parameters for the 16-dimensional input case and seven discrete actions.
